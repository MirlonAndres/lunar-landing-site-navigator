>>> COPIAR Y PEGAR EN EL FORMULARIO (INGLES - 168 palabras):
The lunar south pole is humanity's gateway for sustainable presence under NASA's Artemis program, but presents severe hazards: extreme slopes, hazardous crater rims, and grazing solar elevations (<1.5° above the horizon) where conventional horizontal solar panels receive near-zero irradiance.

We developed Lunar Landing Site Navigator, an integrated aerospace decision-support platform combining Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA), an interactive 3D WebGL Digital Twin, and an edge-controlled 5-DOF robotic arm. 

Using NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) datasets—LOLA altimetry, Diviner thermal maps, LEND neutron hydrogen data, and JPL Horizons celestial ephemerides—our system evaluates candidate Artemis regions and isolates the Top 5 Safe Touchdown Footprints (slope <5°, roughness <0.15m) on Connecting Ridge. 

Crucially, our active 5-DOF robotic solar tracker uses real-time inverse kinematics to keep the photovoltaic panel perpendicular to the grazing polar Sun, delivering a breakthrough 50.1x energy multiplication factor (15,795 Wh vs 315 Wh over 7 days) compared to fixed horizontal panels. The platform features sub-150ms bidirectional hardware-in-the-loop teleoperation via an ESP32-S3 microcontroller and cloud WebSockets.

CAMPO 4: INFORMACION DEL PROYECTO - DETALLES DEL PROYECTO * (Project Details)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
>>> COPIAR Y PEGAR EN EL FORMULARIO (INGLES):

### 1. What does it do and how does it work?
Lunar Landing Site Navigator is an integrated mission control platform that tackles lunar south polar landing and power starvation hazards for NASA's Artemis program:
- **Orbital Planetary Analysis**: Ingests high-resolution NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) LOLA laser altimeter digital elevation models (DEM), LRO Diviner thermal radiometer brightness maps, and LRO LEND epithermal neutron hydrogen indicators.
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)**: Evaluates 4 candidate Artemis landing sites (Connecting Ridge, North Peak, Malapert Mountain, Faustini Rim A) using an objective weighting framework combining slope stability (35%), solar illumination continuity (25%), Direct-to-Earth communication line of sight (20%), water-ice proximity (10%), and thermal extremes (10%).
- **Autonomous Safe Zone Delineation**: Filters topographic hazards (Sobel/Horn gradient slopes and RMS surface roughness) to pinpoint the Top 5 Safe Touchdown Footprints (100 m radius, slope < 5°, roughness < 0.15 m).
- **Interactive 3D WebGL Digital Twin (Three.js)**: Transitions seamlessly from a macro-scale 3D Moon globe with real LROC optical albedo down to a 256x256 LOLA surface mesh, with dynamic shadow rendering and animated parabolic lander relocation.
- **5-DOF Solar-Tracking Robotic Arm**: Executes real-time closed-form inverse kinematics (IK) constrained by anatomical safe joint limits, projecting a visual 5-ray alignment frustum that maintains the solar panel perpendicular to the polar Sun.
- **Hardware-In-The-Loop (HIL) Teleoperation**: Microsecond-responsive dual-core FreeRTOS firmware running on an ESP32-S3 microcontroller coupled to a PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM servo controller over I2C, streaming live photovoltaic ADC telemetry and receiving joint commands over secure cloud WebSockets (<150 ms latency).

### 2. What benefits does it offer?
- **The 50.1x Energy Harvest Multiplier**: Near the lunar south pole, solar elevation oscillates between 0.8° and 1.5°. A fixed horizontal solar panel intercepts a fraction proportional to sin(1.1°) ≈ 0.0192, yielding merely 315 Wh across 7 days (causing battery exhaustion in 18 hours). Our active 5-DOF tracking arm delivers full normal irradiance (1,361 W/m²), yielding 15,795 Wh—a 50.1-fold energy increase that keeps lander survival heaters active and sustains battery State of Charge above 23.8%.
- **Landing Hazard Elimination**: Prevents catastrophic lander tip-over or structural landing gear failure by autonomously excluding steep crater rims and high-relief boulder zones.
- **Immediate Scientific Return**: Pinpoints safe touchdown zones within 5.6 km of cryogenic PSR water-ice cold traps (<40 K), maximizing In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) sample returns.

### 3. What is the expected impact?
The platform serves as an operational blueprint for Artemis lunar surface architectures, human landers, and uncrewed robotic precursors. By converting a critical power vulnerability into a continuous energy harvest and integrating digital twin simulations with physical edge hardware, it significantly lowers mission risk, maximizes robotic survival time across the lunar day/night cycle, and demonstrates low-latency remote teleoperation.

### 4. What tools, languages, hardware, and software were used?
- **Software & Backend**: Python 3.12, FastAPI (asynchronous REST & WebSocket server), Uvicorn ASGI, NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, PyArrow, Tifffile.
- **Frontend & 3D Engine**: Three.js (r128), OrbitControls, HTML5, CSS3 NASA Dark-Theme, modern ES6+ JavaScript, WebGL 2.0.
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Google Cloud Run (containerized Docker deployment with session affinity and TLS encryption).
- **Embedded Hardware**: ESP32-S3 Dual-Core Xtensa LX7 @ 240 MHz, PCA9685 16-Channel 12-bit PWM I2C driver (SDA: GPIO 8, SCL: GPIO 9), 5x high-torque metal-gear servos, photovoltaic voltage divider sensor (GPIO 1 ADC).
- **Mechanical CAD & Manufacturing**: Dassault Systèmes SolidWorks (complete assemblies .SLDASM and parts .SLDPRT), STL 3D printing slicing and fabrication.

### 5. In what sense is the project creative?
We bridged the gap between pure data science and physical robotics: rather than treating lunar landing selection as a static map exercise, we created an active cyber-physical system. The combination of closed-form inverse kinematics with an interactive 5-ray visual alignment frustum directly proves the mathematical physics of polar solar tracking, while physical hardware synchronizes with the digital twin in real time across the cloud.

### 6. What factors did the team take into account?
- **Astronomical Geometry**: Modeled exact topocentric solar vectors and direct Earth visibility profiles using NASA JPL Horizons ephemerides.
- **Cryogenic Survival**: Integrated LRO Diviner thermal measurements (<40 K cold traps) to balance solar power availability with thermal preservation.
- **Mechanical & Electrical Constraints**: Programmed strict servo angle boundaries (Base 25°–155°, Shoulder 35°–115°, Elbow 40°–125°, Wrist 40°–135°, Panel 30°–150°) with soft ramps to prevent mechanical binding, servo stall, and electrical brownouts.
- **Zero-Trust Security**: Sanitized all credentials, using clean environment variables (.env) and public cloud endpoints without hardcoded tokens.

>>> ENUMERAR CADA RECURSO CON SU NOMBRE Y URL COMPLETA:

1. NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) - LOLA Altimeter DEM
   URL: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/lola.htm
   Descripcion: Polar stereographic digital elevation models used for topographic slope, surface roughness, 3D terrain mesh generation, and touchdown hazard detection.

2. NASA LRO Diviner Lunar Radiometer Experiment
   URL: https://diviner.ucla.edu/
   Descripcion: Lunar polar brightness temperature datasets and cryogenic cold trap mapping (<40 K and <110 K) for thermal hazard and ISRU volatiles assessment.

3. NASA LRO Lunar Exploration Neutron Detector (LEND)
   URL: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/lend.htm
   Descripcion: Epithermal neutron suppression data used as an indicator of subsurface hydrogen and water-ice equivalent abundance.

4. NASA JPL Horizons On-Line Solar System Data Service
   URL: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/
   Descripcion: Topocentric celestial ephemerides for the lunar south pole providing 7-day high-precision Sun and Earth azimuth, elevation, and line-of-sight vectors.

5. NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) - CGI Moon Kit
   URL: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
   Descripcion: Global photographic color basemaps and displacement maps from LROC and LOLA for the 3D WebGL planetary digital twin globe.
